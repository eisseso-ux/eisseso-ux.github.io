About Me

I like to tell people my favorite hobby is “hobbies.” Since I was a little girl I’ve always been happiest making things. I received a BA in Studio Art from Wheaton College, and now split my time between making and selling stuffed sea creatures, designing and altering clothing, taking illustration or design commissions, and coaching track and field.

When I’m not working you might find me outside: backpacking, camping, tidepooling, hiking, or swimming. I love anything hands-on sewing, crochet, jewelry making and beading, carving, ceramics, furniture flipping and building, baking, gardening. Every time I pick up something new — whether a tool, a recipe, or a new medium — it's to bring me around friends, out of my head, and into my life.

If this sounds like something you would like to partner with Olivia on, please reach out about commissions or collaborations!
