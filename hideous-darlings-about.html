People are idiots. We want so badly to be taken seriously and are terrified to be laughed at, to be the weird one, the awkward one, the judged one. But life is both misreable, and full of joy. No one is "normal". My art depicts the experience of humanness as the magnificent enigma that it is. Humanness is being depressed, in love, broken, strong, soft, tough, beautiful, dirty, obsessed, brilliant, stupid, bad and good, all at once. Many long seasons of darkness have forged my deepest conviction that my pain and my joy must coexist in harmony.

Reflecting on periods of very specific discomfort and disgust in my own body, my work became its own fleshy community, writhing in pain, suspended, and tied. Simultaneuously this work became a meditative practice of dedicating attention and crasftmanship to each piece and the recycled materails they came from. As the Lord loves and intricately crafts every creature on earth, I honor each piece from the mostly cuddly to the most foul. Within the broken, deformed and unisghtly is where redemption is possible. I want my art to comfort even as it disturbs. My work is childlike and earnest, and, I hope, an invitation for you to let your guard down for a moment. There is something reassuring abou the absurd. We all have crooked and rotten pieces... some of us conceal them better than others. By creating these twisted creatures, I am making physical symbols that are mirrors for our own flaws.

So welcome to the cast! You too are just as ugly, twisted, and beloved.
